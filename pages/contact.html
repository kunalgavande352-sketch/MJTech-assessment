In an emergency

MJTech does not provide emergency response or individualized medical care. If you think you are having a medical emergency, call 911.

Send a message

Please do not include Social Security numbers, insurance IDs, or payment details.

How to send a useful correction

1

Point to the passage

Give the guide title or URL and quote the sentence in question.

2

Say what is wrong

Explain the error, outdated figure, or missing context.

3

Include a source

A link to a government page, guideline, or peer-reviewed study is ideal.

4

Leave out personal health details

We only need the information, not your medical history.
