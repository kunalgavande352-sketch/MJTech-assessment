Editorial Guidelines

How to send a useful correction

1

Identify the Specific Passage

Provide the guide title or URL and quote the exact sentence or parameter in question.

2

Explain the Discrepancy

Clarify whether the figure is outdated, miscontextualized, or contrary to newer findings.

3

Attach Primary Documentation

A direct link to a peer-reviewed publication or government public health agency statement is ideal.

4

Preserve Your Privacy

Never submit your own laboratory tests, medical charts, or private records.
