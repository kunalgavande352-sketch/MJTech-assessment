Our editorial purpose

MJTech exists to make general health information easier to understand. We write about sleep, circadian timing, metabolic health, hydration, and healthy longevity, and we build simple tools that help readers think through everyday decisions.

We are not a clinic, hospital, pharmacy, or medical provider. Our job is to explain, not to diagnose or treat.

Research sourcing standards

Sources are chosen in a rough order of reliability for general readers.

Government health agencies

NIH institutes, CDC, NHLBI, MedlinePlus, and the FDA when relevant.

Established clinical guidance

Recognized professional organizations such as the American Heart Association and the American Academy of Sleep Medicine.

Peer-reviewed research

Used sparingly, with the study type and its limits described.

Corrections and updates

If you spot an error, an outdated figure, or a broken source, send it through the Contact page or email support@mjtech.com.

We check the point against the cited source and, where needed, against newer guidance. When evidence changes, the affected guide is reviewed and revised.

What we do not claim

  • We do not claim to employ physicians or other licensed clinicians.
  • We do not operate a medical review board.
  • We do not hold health certifications or accreditations.
  • We do not claim institutional affiliations or endorsements.
