Our Mission & Ethics

About MJTech

An independent digital wellness research platform built on a clear standard: say what authoritative sources say, cite where figures originate, and be completely explicit about clinical uncertainty.

Editorial Rigor

  • Primary source verified
  • Threshold figures cross-checked
  • Study limitations declared

Our editorial purpose

MJTech was created to make health science comprehensible and actionable for everyday life. We examine circadian biology, metabolic biomarkers, daily physical habits, and healthy longevity. Alongside our briefs, we build transparent browser calculators to help readers explore routines and prepare for doctor visits.

We are not a clinic, diagnostic laboratory, or telehealth provider. Our role is strictly educational: to translate peer-reviewed consensus and help you ask better questions with your licensed healthcare provider.

Evidence Hierarchy

Research sourcing standards

We evaluate health literature according to strict clinical credibility standards.

Public Health Agencies

We reference guidance published by the CDC, NIH Institutes (NIGMS, NHLBI, NIA), MedlinePlus, and the FDA.

Highest consensus benchmark

Clinical Medical Societies

Peer-reviewed clinical recommendations from recognized bodies including the American Heart Association and Sleep Research Society.

Specialist clinical guidance

Peer-Reviewed Trials

Controlled human intervention trials, clearly categorized so readers never confuse observational correlation with clinical cause.

Contextualized research

Corrections and scholarly review

If you discover an outdated number, a broken link, or an emerging clinical update that affects our guides, please notify our editorial desk through our contact form or directly at support@mjtech.com.

All corrections are investigated against primary literature. When new medical guidelines are approved by major organizations, relevant guides are promptly updated with revision notices.

Ethical transparency

  • We do not accept paid placements, sponsored clinical ratings, or affiliate commissions on health supplements.
  • We do not claim licensed clinicians on staff or institutional endorsements.
  • Our calculators run entirely locally in your browser to respect your total privacy.
