Wellness Tools

Metabolic health reflection

Evidence-based reflection prompt — not a diagnostic risk score.

Review key lifestyle markers to formulate constructive discussion questions for your physician.

Circadian sleep window estimator

Calculate bedtime targets anchored to your waking hour.

Works backward from your target wake-up time, accounting for recommended adult sleep ranges and sleep latency.

Hydration planner

Calculates baseline daily fluid goals calibrated to physiological variables.

Provides a flexible intake estimate based on body weight, climate, and exercise. If your clinician has advised fluid restrictions, always defer to their clinical plan.

Preventive habit planner

Generate a structured question agenda for your wellness exam.

Select the topics you wish to review at your routine physical. Generates clear questions and reminders to take along.
