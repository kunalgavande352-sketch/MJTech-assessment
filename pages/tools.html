Your toolkit

Wellness Tools

Four simple utilities for thinking through sleep timing, hydration, metabolic habits, and appointment preparation. They show their assumptions, run in your browser, and save nothing.

General estimates and reflections only. Not a diagnosis or personal medical advice.

Jump to a tool

  • Metabolic health reflection
  • Circadian sleep window
  • Hydration planner
  • Preventive habit planner

Metabolic health reflection

This is a structured reflection, not a validated index and not a risk score. It never predicts diabetes, heart disease, or any other condition.

Circadian sleep window estimator

Enter when you need to wake up and how long you want to sleep. The tool works backward to a bedtime, including when it falls after midnight.

Hydration planner

A rough planning range based on body weight, activity, and climate. You can then edit the result. If a clinician has told you to limit fluids, do not use this tool to change your intake.

Preventive habit planner

Pick topics for a routine wellness appointment. The tool builds a list of things to bring and questions to ask. Avoid typing sensitive personal details.
