Reading research without confusing association and causation

When two things occur together, it does not mean one caused the other. Before changing a habit because of a headline, check what kind of study it came from.

Association

Two things move together. Observational studies mostly show this.

Confounding

A third factor, such as income or exercise, may explain the link.

Reverse causation

Illness may change habits, instead of habits causing illness.

Trials

Randomized trials test cause more directly, within their limits.
