Scientific Literacy

Distinguishing correlation from clinical cause

Headlines frequently sensationalize lifestyle studies. Understanding trial design helps you evaluate whether a finding applies to real life.

Observational Link

Two variables trend together across a population. This reveals potential patterns, but does not prove one caused the other.

Hypothesis generation

Confounding Factors

Underlying variables such as household income, leisure time, or sleep quality often explain the apparent benefit.

Hidden variables

Reverse Causation

Early asymptomatic disease may alter daily habits first, creating an illusion that the habit provoked the condition.

Randomized Trials
