Got Questions?

Frequently asked questions

Transparent information regarding our tools, data privacy, and editorial scope.

What is MJTech's mission?

MJTech provides evidence-based health research briefs and privacy-first wellness tools to help individuals understand sleep, metabolic health, and longevity science without commercial hype or alarmist framing.

Are MJTech tools medical diagnostic calculators?

No. Our tools are educational planning aids and reflections designed to prompt thoughtful discussions with your healthcare provider. They do not diagnose conditions or formulate medical prescriptions.

Is any personal health information saved?

Never. All calculations run strictly client-side inside your browser session. We do not store, track, or transmit any numbers or inputs you enter.

Where do your guides get their research?

We pull directly from published public health agency guidelines (CDC, NIH, AHA, NHLBI) and peer-reviewed journals. Every guide lists its source citations clearly.

How can I submit a question or correction?

We welcome reader feedback and scholarly citations. You can reach our editorial team directly through our contact page or at support@mjtech.com.
